Important source limitation:
The supplied upstream revision does not contain the complete Music library/player UI.
MusicBrowserActivity and MusicPicker are empty placeholders, and MediaPlaybackService
contains an empty MediaSession callback. The Android 10 port adds only a small local-song
launcher/list so the package can be opened as an installed app. The original AudioPreview
implementation and its resources are retained. This is not feature parity with a complete
legacy Music app; full playlists, artist/album browsing, a persistent background playback
engine, and original full-screen animations cannot be recovered from this source snapshot.

Compatibility target:
- minSdk 29 (Android 10)
- targetSdk 29 (Android 10 legacy external-storage behavior)
- compileSdk 35
- Java 8 source compatibility
